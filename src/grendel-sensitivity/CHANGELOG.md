## 2.0 — 2026-09-30

v2 of the curves: the fixes below, BC5, and exHad decays for every
benchmark. The tables are in `curves/data/grendel_v2` (the v1 tables stay in
`curves/data/grendel_v1`).

- The v2 curves use exHad's decays (arXiv:2609.16104) for the HNL, BC4, BC5
  and BC10, each template carrying exHad's own lifetime; the recipe, the
  input provenance and the changes against v1 are in
  `curves/data/grendel_v2/README.md`. The largest: the HNL lower edge at
  3.4–3.6 GeV falls to 0.3–0.6 of v1 (with the B_c fix) and U_τ reaches
  4.0 GeV; the BC4 upper edge falls to 0.23–0.35 at 2–3.5 GeV, the exHad
  scalar width being 1.85–2.3× the Winkler digitisation there; BC10 moves by
  less than 7 % on its lower edge.
- BC5: the dark scalar with the quartic coupling fixed by BR(h → SS) = 0.01,
  the PBC 2025 benchmark plane (m_S, sin²θ) from 0.14 GeV to m_h/2
  (`grendel.models.scalar.scan_bc5`). Production adds h → S S over every
  SM Higgs mode (MadGraph + Pythia 8 kinematics, YR4 normalisation),
  b → X_s S S and B_s → S S (SensCalc rates) to the BC4 rows; the scan
  carries a per-row `coupling_power` (sixth CSV column, default 1) so
  coupling-independent production and the sin²θ lifetime share one yield
  curve; the decay model continues above 7.5 GeV with the EventCalc
  tables (S → b b̄ as a two-track proxy). BC4, BC10 and HNL numbers are
  unchanged.
- BC5 comparison panel (`python -m curves.plot paper --benchmark bc5`):
  the existing exclusion and the ANUBIS, CODEX-b, MATHUSLA40 and SHiP
  projections of the PBC 2025 BC5 figure, read from the figure's vector
  paths (`curves.tools.vector_bc5`, `curves/data/bc5/`). The SHiP BC5
  cross-check file under `curves/data/bc4/` is superseded by that
  projection; the FASER2 BC5 reference is re-extracted without the label
  leader line it carried as a stray second chain.
- The BC5 exclusion adds the ATLAS/CMS h → S S long-lived-particle searches
  as translated to BR(h → SS) = 0.01 by arXiv:2509.02564, from its data
  (`curves.tools.lhc_bc5`, `curves/data/bc5/existing/`), drawn with the
  PBC 2025 region in one opaque gray.
- Fixes from the post-release review that move the published curves (the
  v1 tables stay in `curves/data/grendel_v1`):
  - B_c normalisation `SIGMA_BC_PB` 0.9e6 → 1.64e6 pb, the LHCb anchor it
    cites (f(B_c⁻)/(f(B⁻) + f(B̄⁰)) = 3.7e-3, both charges); the HNL lower
    edge deepens by up to 39 % where B_c matters (U_τ above 1.5 GeV, U_e and
    U_μ above 2.8 GeV).
  - BC10 Pythia templates shower and hadronise the partonic ALP decays
    (93–97 % above 2.2 GeV) with `meMode` 91; v1's `meMode` 0 (still
    `--partonic-mode minimal`) turned them into the minimal number of
    hadrons. The peak yield at 2.2–3.3 GeV rises by 9 % (up to 17 %) and
    the island reaches 3.36 GeV (v1: 3.28).
  - BC4 decay-model alternate (`chpt_spectator`): the gluonic width runs
    α_s at four loops from α_s(M_Z) = 0.1180 instead of freezing it at 0.30.
  - The ray cast keeps every in-volume segment of the curved gallery, and the
    reconstruction tests the fiducial volume against the ray-cast mesh
    (`grendel.geometry.fiducial`, vertical-ray parity with exact orientation
    predicates, so a ray through a shared edge or vertex counts once): the
    analytic test accepted ~0.4 % extra volume at the bends, 0.2–0.7 %
    optimistic in N_sig.
- `scan --resume` keeps a row only if it was computed with the same settings
  and package code (a mismatch is an error) from input files with the same
  SHA-256 (a point's four-vectors and templates; a changed file, or a run
  that recorded no digests, is recomputed), and picks up the partial
  checkpoint. A scan that produces no row fails (`NoResultsError`, exit code 1
  from the model CLIs) and moves any earlier `sensitivity.csv` aside to
  `*.stale`. The geometry cache is keyed by a SHA-256 of the directions, the
  origin and the mesh it was cast for; a cache it cannot verify is recast.
- Comparison layers: the HNL BBN layer is the single-Majorana bound of Chen
  and Zhang (arXiv:2410.07343, 95 % CL), replacing Sabti et al., whose
  bounds do not apply to a single Majorana HNL; the BC4 SHiP curve is traced
  from the ECN3 figure it is cited to; CODEX-b's HNL curves are converted to
  Majorana on both edges (N_M(U²) = ½ N_D(2U²), an approximate recast whose
  accuracy `curves/PROVENANCE.md` states); the BC10 beam-dump fill is
  clipped to its source range; the raw HNLimits copies convert their MeV
  masses to GeV.
- `grendel.band.hnl.kaon_band` assembles the kaon escape-distance band that
  `plot_money` draws; campaign tooling fixes (FONLL grid validation, the
  BC4/BC10/HNL band bookkeeping, the Linux FONLL build, package data, CI's
  full-history checkout).


- Cite FairShip by its archived DOI (10.5281/zenodo.18020628) in
  `CITATION.cff`, `THIRD_PARTY.md`, `README.md` and the pin.
- Put the two FairShip-derived files — the Pythia 8 decay-table setup in
  `grendel/models/hnl/templates/fairship_decay.py` and the decay-selection
  config — under FairShip's LGPL-3.0-or-later, with SPDX headers, the licence
  text in `LICENSES/`, and the boundary recorded in `THIRD_PARTY.md`. The

## 1.0 — 2026-09-21

First release: the sensitivity machinery of arXiv:2609.00152.

- Detector geometry, two-track vertex reconstruction and the acceptance
  Monte Carlo shared by every model.
- One scan driver (`grendel.scan`) with per-model numerical policies for the
  heavy neutral lepton (BC6–BC8), the dark scalar (BC4) and the fermiophilic
  ALP (BC10); islands solved at any number of signal thresholds.
- Production: FONLL heavy-flavour grids and their generator, HNL channels
  (mesons, baryons, kaons, tau, W/Z), inclusive b → X_s S and B → K⁽ⁱ⁾ a.
- Decay templates from exHad, FairShip, Pythia 8 (BC10) and the analytic and proxy backends.
- Uncertainty campaigns (FONLL scale, PDF replicas and heavy-quark masses;
  decay-model, B_c and numerical-control nuisances) and their combination.
- The comparison-curve renderer with the digitised reference curves and
  the tools that produced them.
